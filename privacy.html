Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This tool generates an HTML email signature — including a ready-made GITAM-styled option for verified GITAM students and staff, and fully independent, custom-branded signatures for any other organization or personal use. This page explains what it is, what information it collects, and how that information is used.

About this tool

This is an independently built project, not an official GITAM product. It is not created, operated, or endorsed by GITAM (Deemed to be University). It was inspired by an internal signature tool GITAM already provides its own staff, and was built as a general-purpose email signature generator that anyone can use for their own organization, with their own banner image, icon colors, and branding.

As a courtesy to actual GITAM students and staff specifically, this tool also offers one ready-made "GITAM Signature" option in GITAM's familiar visual style, so GITAM users don't have to design their own from scratch. Because that particular template references GITAM's identity, it's only available to accounts verified via Google sign-in on @gitam.in, @student.gitam.edu, or @alumni.gitam.edu — that restriction exists specifically to keep the GITAM-styled option limited to people actually affiliated with GITAM, rather than open to anyone. Every account, GITAM-affiliated or not, can also create any number of fully independent signatures with their own name, banner, and icon colors, entirely unrelated to GITAM.

If you're affiliated with GITAM and have concerns about this tool's use of GITAM's name or visual identity, please get in touch using the contact details below and we'll address it.

What we collect

When you sign in and fill in your details, we store what you enter yourself: your name, program/designation, department, school/organization, campus/location, mobile number, website, social media links, and a photo you upload — plus, for a custom (non-GITAM) signature, your own banner image and icon color choice.

Signing in

GITAM accounts sign in with Google, restricted as described above; we only receive your name and email address from Google to identify your account — nothing else from your Google account is accessed, read, or stored. The one admin account signs in separately with a password, unrelated to Google.

How your information is used

Your details and photo are used solely to render the email signature(s) shown to you on this site, which you choose to copy and use in your own email client (e.g. Gmail). We do not send emails on your behalf, and we do not share, sell, or use your information for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your data and images are stored with Supabase, the database and file-storage provider this tool runs on. A photo or custom banner is stored at a public, unguessable link so that email clients (like Gmail) can load it when displaying your signature — this is what allows a photo to update automatically in signatures you've already sent, similar to how other email-signature tools work.

Your control over your data

You can update or replace any of your details, photo, or banner at any time by logging back in. To have your data removed, contact us using the email below.

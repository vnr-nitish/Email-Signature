Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This tool helps GITAM students and staff generate an HTML email signature. This page explains what information it collects and how it's used.

What we collect

When you sign in and fill in your profile, we store the details you enter yourself: your name, program/designation, department, school, campus/location, mobile number, website, social media links, and a profile photo you upload.

Signing in with Google

Sign-in is restricted to @gitam.in, @student.gitam.edu, and @alumni.gitam.edu Google accounts. We only receive your name and email address from Google to identify your account — nothing else from your Google account is accessed, read, or stored.

How your information is used

Your details and photo are used solely to render the email signature shown to you on the Dashboard, which you choose to copy and use in your own email client (e.g. Gmail). We do not send emails on your behalf, and we do not share, sell, or use your information for advertising.

Where it's stored

Your profile data and photo are stored with Supabase, the database and file-storage provider this tool runs on. Your profile photo is stored at a public, unguessable link so that email clients (like Gmail) can load it when displaying your signature — this is what allows your photo to update automatically in signatures you've already sent, similar to how other university email-signature tools work.

Your control over your data

You can update or replace any of your details or your photo at any time by logging back in. To have your data removed, contact us using the email below.
